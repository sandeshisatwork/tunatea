Our Story

TUNA TEA began in the hills of eastern Nepal, where our founder's grandmother brewed her first pot over a wood fire. Today we blend that same warmth with modern care — hand-picking leaves, roasting in small batches, and packaging fresh for every order.

Every cup carries the story of the farmers we work with, the morning mist of the hills, and the simple joy of slowing down. We're proud to be a Nepali-owned, family-run tea house.
